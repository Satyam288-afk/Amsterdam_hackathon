## Sambhaash AI — AI Revenue Recovery

Sambhaash AI is an AI revenue-recovery agent for overdue B2B receivables. It detects revenue at risk, diagnoses payment delays, selects the appropriate recovery intervention, and executes bounded multilingual voice and WhatsApp workflows. When customers promise to pay, Sambhaash records the commitment and pauses unnecessary outreach; failed promises trigger controlled escalation. The system tracks every action and measures recovered revenue across a reproducible synthetic batch benchmark.

### What the demo proves

- Explainable invoice risk scoring from overdue days, outstanding amount, prior attempts, and missed promises.
- Controlled cause classification: payment delay, invoice dispute, approval delay, payment failure, customer unreachable, promise missed, or unknown.
- Deterministic policy selection; the LLM cannot invent financial actions or bypass safeguards.
- Hard stop rules: payment received, active promise-to-pay, opt-out, three automated attempts, and one voice call per day.
- Multilingual recovery interaction: `Friday ko payment kar denge` stores a date and amount, schedules follow-up, and pauses outreach.
- A code-calculated, clearly labelled synthetic comparison of a generic reminder against the bounded Sambhaash workflow.

### Important disclosure

The submission demo uses fictional invoice records clearly marked **DEMO DATA**. Benchmark figures are generated from a documented synthetic assumption model and are not claims of real merchant recovery performance.
